# Research & Project Images

Place architecture diagrams, screenshots, and cover images here.

## Folders
- `public/images/research/` — project cover images and architecture diagrams
- `public/images/uploads/` — auto-generated by Decap CMS media library

## Naming convention
- `agent-minimiser-cover.jpg`
- `diagram-intelligence-architecture.png`

## How images appear
- Research cards on `/research` homepage preview
- Research case study pages (`/research/[slug]`)
- Award cards on `/awards`

## Supported formats
JPG, PNG, WEBP. PDFs are supported for certificates but convert to images for best display.
